# Leagues (migrations `20261022180000`, `20261022190000`, `20261022200000`, `20261022210000`, `20261022220000`, `20261022230000`, `20261023090000`, `20261023100000`, `20261023110000`, `20261023120000`, `20261023130000`, `20261023193153`, `20261023201147`, `20261023205736`, `20261023213048`, `20261023221049`, `20261024084837`, `20261024094617`, `20261024102616`, `20261025072000`)

> Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 23 Oct 2026. The settled design for what is still to build is `docs/design/leagues.md`.

Built to `docs/design/leagues.md`. **This is the structure only** — the league, its seasons, the teams, who is entered, who runs it. No fixtures, lineups, captain's scorecard or standings yet; each is its own step. **Step 2 added the pages and step 3 the fixtures** (file map above): everything below is reachable from `/leagues/`, and was tested on a throwaway Postgres, the pages in headless Chromium against it. Still missing: lineups, the frames themselves, the captain's scorecard and standings — steps 4 to 6.

- **Tables:** `tbl_seasons`, `tbl_leagues`, `tbl_league_seasons`, `tbl_teams`, `tbl_league_season_entrants`. Plural, matching `tbl_tournaments`; the design document's singular names are not the built ones.
  - `tbl_leagues` carries `rules` (validated by `private.tdc_check_match_rules`) and a nullable `venue_id`, as tournaments do — anyone may run a league, and a club's staff can rescue one. `tbl_league_seasons.rules_overrides` is the one place a **partial** ruleset is allowed, because it is merged over the league's before anything reads it.
  - **`private.tdc_rules_for_league_season` is the one place that merge is done** (`league.rules || overrides`, flat keys so the shallow merge is exact). Matches freeze the result at creation, as they always have. Creating or updating a season validates the *merged* result; changing a league's rules re-checks every season's, so an invalid combination is refused where it is made, not found at a season's first match.
  - **Teams do not persist across seasons.** A team belongs to one league season. `team_lineage_id` is a plain shared id linking successive incarnations (no foreign key — there is no lineage row to point at). `tdc_create_team(…, p_continues_team_id)` sets it, linking the earlier team retrospectively; one incarnation per lineage per season.
  - **The roster is the entrant row and nothing else.** `tbl_league_season_entrants.team_id` is who is in the team *now*; a captain is `is_captain` on the same row; unique per (season, player), so a transfer is an update and drops the captaincy unless asked again. The design called "who is in team X" the question with two answers (roster, and who actually played); a separate member table would have added a third. **A composite foreign key `(team_id, league_season_id)` stops a player being put on another season's team by any path**, not only through the functions.
  - **`tbl_league_season_entrants` has RLS on, no policy and no grant.** It carries payment and the director's notes, so it is read only through `tdc_league_season_entrants` (director's list, gated) and `tdc_team_roster` (names and captaincy, open to signed-in players). Built this way deliberately because `tbl_tournament_entrants` was not — see Tournaments above.
- **No status column on a league season** — *superseded 24 Oct* (below): the status is now the league director's to set. The original reasoning was that the tournament one sat at `draft` for two days because nothing wrote it, so a league season's status would be **derived** from its fixtures. `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- **Roles.** `tbl_competition_roles` gained `league_season_id`; the old "has a tournament" check became "has exactly one of the two", and a partial unique index covers the league rows (the tournament constraint cannot — a null `tournament_id` makes every row distinct). The `director` role type is **shared** (LD and TD are both rank 100, and rank is unique); `tdc_league_officials` labels it "League director" and the lookup row is untouched, so the tournament officials list reads as before.
  - **A captain is not a competition role.** It is per team, so it lives on the entrant row. `leagues.md` §7 pointed at the roles table; that pointer was imprecise.
  - `tdc_remove_competition_role` still reads `tournament_id` only, so handed a league role's id it refuses with tournament wording. Use `tdc_remove_league_role`.
- **Access:** `private.tdc_can_run_league(league_season)` — the season's director, or staff of the venue the league belongs to. `private.tdc_can_manage_league(league)` — who made it, a director of any of its seasons, or venue staff; separate because a new season has no director yet. `tdc_create_league_season` makes the caller its director in the same transaction.
- **Functions:** `tdc_create_league`, `tdc_update_league`, `tdc_create_league_season`, `tdc_update_league_season`, `tdc_create_team`, `tdc_update_team`, `tdc_delete_team`, `tdc_add_league_entrant`, `tdc_set_league_entrant`, `tdc_remove_league_entrant`, `tdc_add_league_role`, `tdc_remove_league_role`; reads `tdc_may_run_league`, `tdc_league_season_info`, `tdc_league_season_teams`, `tdc_team_roster`, `tdc_league_season_rosters` (every team's roster in a season in one call, the same rows and order as `tdc_team_roster`; the Season tab's read, `20261117041517`), `tdc_league_season_entrants`, `tdc_league_officials`, `tdc_my_leagues`.
  - `tdc_add_league_entrant` is idempotent in the tournament way: re-adding brings a withdrawal back and fills in a missing team, but never moves a placed player and never removes a captaincy.
  - A season's period (`tbl_seasons`) can be shared between leagues; `tdc_update_league_season` refuses to move its dates when another league uses it.
- **Matches now have their teams.** The foreign keys on `tbl_matches.home_team_id` / `away_team_id` promised since 14 Oct, `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, plus `tbl_matches_teams_differ`. Nothing creates a team match yet and the match page still refuses to load one.
- **`private.tdc_merge_players` was missing two tables, and one of them broke it.** `tbl_match_events.player_id` is `ON DELETE RESTRICT` and was never moved, so a signup record that had ever submitted or answered a card made the walk-in claim **fail outright** with a raw foreign-key error — reproduced against the production definition before the fix. `tbl_deleted_matches` (three player columns) was also missing. Both are moved now, along with the league entrants, and a record already entered in the same league season as the one it is being joined to is refused with a plain message (defensive since 190000: an accountless record can no longer be an entrant). Built by patching the live definition (the diff against it is additions only), not retyping it.
- **Who may be a league player, and who controls a team (migration `20261022190000`, UV 22 Oct).**
  - **A walk-in or guest can never be a league player.** Walk-ins are for business operations, tournaments and matches. `tdc_add_league_entrant` refuses an accountless player with a plain message, and a `BEFORE INSERT` trigger on the entrants table (`private.tdc_league_entrant_needs_account`) refuses it by any other path. **Insert-only on purpose:** `tdc_merge_players` repoints entrants at a walk-in record that only gains its `user_id` at the end of the merge, so an update guard would trip on it. A consequence: the merge's "both records in the same league season" guard can no longer be reached. It stays as a defence, and is not tested.
  - **Every league player confirms their own place** (`confirmed_at` on the entrant row). Only `tdc_confirm_league_entry` sets it, and only for the caller; nobody confirms for someone else and the table cannot be written directly. A withdrawn player added back, or reactivated, must confirm again. `tdc_withdraw_league_entry` is the player's own way out; a team's only captain cannot withdraw.
  - **Adding a player notifies them** (category `league`, kind `league_entry_added`, linking to `/leagues/season/?s=<id>`, the page where they confirm; it was null until the page existed), once per add, and again when a withdrawn player is brought back.
  - **Only the league director creates teams. Captains control them:** `private.tdc_can_control_team` (the director, or an active *confirmed* captain of that team) gates `tdc_team_claim_player`, `tdc_team_release_player`, `tdc_team_set_captain` and the now-widened `tdc_update_team` (rename). A captain claims only a player who is in the season, active, **confirmed** and on no team. A team's last captain cannot be released or removed by anyone but the director, through `tdc_set_league_entrant`.
  - **Players can still be added mid-season** by the director. They arrive unconfirmed and unplaced, and a captain claims them once they have confirmed. Captains inviting players themselves is a later conversation, **not built**.
  - **Readiness** (`private.tdc_league_ready`, `tdc_league_season_readiness`): at least one active player, every active player confirmed; withdrawn and inactive are not waited for. Also reports who is still waiting and how many are unplaced. **The fixtures step (3) must refuse to run unless this is true.** Readable by the director and by captains.
  - `tdc_team_roster`, `tdc_league_season_entrants` and `tdc_my_leagues` gained the confirmation (`confirmed`, `confirmed_at`, `my_status`); they were dropped and recreated, so their grants were redone.
- **Reads the pages needed (migration `20261022200000`).** `tdc_search_league_players(season, query, limit)` — **accounts only**, gated to whoever runs the season, and returns `entrant_status` so a withdrawn player can be added back and one already in is shown as such. It is separate from the tournament search because that one returns walk-ins and is gated on a tournament. `tdc_league_unplaced(season)` — confirmed, active players on no team, readable by the director and by a confirmed captain, because `tdc_league_season_entrants` is the director's list (payment, notes) and a captain cannot have that. `private.tdc_is_league_captain` is the one place "an active, confirmed captain of some team in this season" is answered. `tdc_add_league_entrant` was patched from its live text for the notification link, nothing else.
- **Fixtures (migration `20261022210000`).** `tbl_league_fixtures` — `league_season_id`, `round`, `position`, `home_team_id`, a nullable `away_team_id`, `match_id`, `scheduled_at`, `notes`. RLS on with a `select … using (true)` policy and a select grant, as `tbl_tournament_fixtures` has: a fixture list is not secret, unlike the entrant list.
  - **Three things make it unlike a tournament draw.** Nothing is derived from a result (a league's whole schedule is known the moment the teams are, so there is no `winner_of` and no cascade); **a bye is a row**, with the home side naming the team sitting out, because "who do we play this week" deserves the answer "nobody" in the same list as every other answer; and the sides are teams.
  - **The composite foreign keys `(home_team_id, league_season_id)` and `(away_team_id, league_season_id)`** make a cross-season fixture impossible by any path, the same guard the entrant row has. A bye can never carry a match (`tbl_league_fixtures_bye_has_no_match`).
  - **`tdc_generate_league_fixtures(season, times_played, first_round_at, days_between, replace, detach_matches)`** is the circle method: one team held still, the rest rotating around it, which gives every pairing exactly once with nobody playing twice in a round. An odd field gets a phantom team, and whoever draws it has a bye. The held-still team's side alternates by round, so it is not at home every week; playing twice replays the same pairings with the sides swapped, which is what makes a double round home-and-away — **after it, every team is at home exactly as often as away**, and each meeting happens once each way. One to four meetings. **On the page (24 Oct)** the meetings are a number labelled "Rounds" (1–4), beside Days between rounds; the first round is the date control plus an hour and a minute stepper (there is no date-and-time control), put back together as local time before sending.
  - **More than one match per team a night, and table numbers (migration `20261024191110`, 24 Oct).** `p_matches_per_round` (1–6): that many passes of the round robin share one night and one round number, positions running on through the night, so each team plays that many times (a bye counts as one). `p_tables_per_match` (0–20) fills `tbl_league_fixtures.table_numbers`, **numbered on through the night** (`20261024194237`, UV): with 2, the night's fixtures are {1,2}, {3,4}, {5,6}… whichever pass they belong to, so no two fixtures of a night share a table and a team playing twice is never on the same table twice. A bye gets none. (`20261024191110` restarted at each pass; replaced the same day.) `tdc_add_league_fixture` and `tdc_set_league_fixture` take `p_table_numbers`; neither invents numbers: the page sends the night's next free ones when adding. `tdc_league_fixtures` returns them. Tests: `032_league_fixture_tables`.
  - **Tables available (migration `20261025082733`, 25 Oct, UV).** `p_tables_available` (null for no limit, else 1–99) caps that numbering: the fixture that reaches the last table gets what is left (two asked, one left: one), and the night's later fixtures get none (`{}`), for the director to set by hand. **Used for that Generate only; nothing is stored** (UV), so Add a fixture still numbers on past it. On the page it sits left of Tables per match, blank meaning no limit. Dropped and recreated for the new parameter, grants redone. Tests: `036_league_tables_available`.
  - **The page's order (25 Oct, UV):** Planner (open), All fixtures, Make the schedule (folded, director only), Add a fixture (its own group, director only; it still adds to the night chosen in the planner).
  - **It refuses unless `private.tdc_league_ready`** — every active player confirmed. That is what confirmation is *for*. It also refuses while any team has no confirmed players, naming them, because a fixture against an empty team cannot be played; and while there are fewer than two teams.
  - **`tdc_create_league_match(fixture)` is the first thing in the system to write a team match** (`home_side_kind = 'team'`). The columns shipped 14 Oct and the foreign keys on 22 Oct. It freezes `private.tdc_rules_for_league_season` onto the match and is idempotent, like the tournament one. **Nothing can score it yet** — the match page refuses to load a team match until the lineups and the scorecard exist — so the page says so when it creates one.
  - **Nothing here notifies anybody**, deliberately: see `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
  - Also `tdc_clear_league_fixtures`, `tdc_add_league_fixture`, `tdc_set_league_fixture`, `tdc_remove_league_fixture`, `tdc_detach_league_match`, and the read `tdc_league_fixtures(season)`, which joins the team names, the match status and the score. A fixture with a match cannot have its teams changed or be removed until the match is taken off it.
  - **A signed match stays on its fixture (migration `20261025072000`, 25 Oct, UV).** Complete means both captains signed. `tdc_detach_league_match` refuses one, and `tdc_clear_league_fixtures` (and so generating again) refuses while the season holds one, confirmed or not. Found when UV took a signed night off its fixture: nothing was lost, but Start then made a fresh match asking for the lineups. The migration put that night back and deleted the empty match, and an older empty one left by a regenerate. **Moving a signed fixture's date is still allowed by the database**, for the LD approvals page (not built); the fixtures page shows a signed fixture with only the card button and read-only tables, and Move all skips it. Tests: `035_league_signed_match_locked`.
  - **Resetting a night (migration `20261118120357`, 18 Nov, UV; DECISIONS 18 Nov, "Resetting a league night…").** `tdc_reset_league_match(fixture)`, for whoever runs the season (`private.tdc_can_run_league`), takes a started night back to how it stood just after Start: the fixture and the **same match row** stay (card links and the short number keep working); its frames (participants cascade), `tbl_frame_edit`, `tbl_frame_change_accept`, every `tbl_match_events` row, `tbl_league_match_order` / `_squad` / `_sides`, any `tbl_match_result_override` and the night's `tbl_notifications` are deleted under `tdc.card_unlock`; the match goes back to `scheduled` with no start, end, referee, first break or break in play, takes the season's current rules (`tdc_rules_for_league_season`) and its `card_version` is bumped. **Events are deleted, not left to lapse**: a signature counts again whenever the card's content matches its fingerprint (`private.tdc_card_state`), so a replayed night could revive one. **Signed nights too** (UV). A fixture with no match is refused. Each reset writes a row to **`tbl_league_match_resets`** (who by account, when, home and away frames, frame count, whether it was signed, and a summary such as "Hosts 2 – Guests 0, 2 frames, signed"); RLS on, no grants, no page reads it yet. **The page:** Reset the night (the ring set's `undo`, danger) on the Schedule page for every started fixture, beside Take the match off, and the only button on a signed one; the question names the score, more firmly when signed. Rules tab: "Resetting a match". Tests: `084_league_night_reset`.
    - **A night off the schedule leaves the profile menu and the live card (migration `20261118122114`, 18 Nov, UV).** Found after the first resets: a night reset then taken off its fixture still showed in the profile menu, because `tdc_my_active` listed every scheduled or in-progress team match of the reader's team, on a fixture or not (`tdc_my_live_matches` with no league given, the same). Both now count a team match only while a `tbl_league_fixtures` row points at it; singles matches unchanged. Not new with the reset: any night taken off its fixture had stayed. Tests: `084`.
  - **Scores and live updates on the fixtures page (25 Oct, UV).** Each side's score sits between its team and the buttons once a frame has a result or the match is finished (on a phone, while a match is played, above the buttons: there is no room beside them). A finished fixture has no buttons but the director's Reset (18 Nov; Enter on it is the button's, not the row's); its rows carry `data-card` and the whole box opens the card. Every match on the schedule still `scheduled` or `in_progress` is watched through one realtime channel on `tbl_matches` updates (every frame write bumps `card_version`), filtered by id up to 20 matches; a change re-reads `tdc_league_fixtures`, waiting while a write, a move or a table save is under way. Finished matches are not watched. Showing the page again or coming back online re-reads.
- **Squads, lineups and the frames (migration `20261022230000`).** Three tables and the act that turns them into a card.
  - `tbl_league_match_squad` — who is available tonight, per side. Open to read, editable all night, because somebody always drops out at six.
  - `tbl_league_match_order` — which player takes which slot. **RLS on, no policy and no grant**: it is the one table holding a secret, and the blind exchange is enforced in `tdc_match_lineup` rather than in a policy clever enough to re-implement the rule somewhere it would then live twice.
  - `tbl_league_match_sides` — per side: locked, when, and `by_cutoff` so the card can say the cutoff did it rather than a captain.
  - **The blind exchange.** `tdc_match_lineup` returns every slot of both sides, so a captain sees the shape of the night, but **withholds the opponent's names until both sides have locked**. An empty slot is never a secret; `hidden` marks a slot that has somebody in it you are not allowed to know yet. The league director sees everything, always.
  - **Frames are created when the second side locks** (`private.tdc_materialise_lineup_frames`, idempotent). Both sides filled → a `pending` frame with its two participants. One side filled → a `complete` frame, `result_event = 'default'`, won by whoever turned up. **Neither filled → no frame; the slot vanishes.** Frames are numbered from 1 with no gaps, while `lineup_slot` keeps the row of the sheet — which is why both columns exist.
  - **`tbl_frames` gained `'pending'`** as a fourth status, and a nullable `lineup_slot`. A frame row used to mean a frame that had at least started; a team card is a grid that exists from the lock. Everything that counts a score already asks for `status = 'complete'`, so a pending frame contributes nothing to any total.
  - **`tbl_frame_participants` needed nothing** — it already existed, already carried `(frame, side, player, play_order)`, and was already understood by `tdc_record_frame`, `tdc_can_edit_match`, `tdc_my_matches` and the walk-in merge. It was built for exactly this.
  - **`by_schedule` needed nothing either**: `private.tdc_break_side_for_frame` already returns null for it, so nothing is derived — which is necessary, because `winner_breaks` and `loser_breaks` cannot be derived in team play at all, where frame 4 routinely finishes before frame 3.
  - **Confirmation does not gate the fixtures (23 Oct).** It did between 22 and 23 Oct. UV, testing a real league: *"It is not a requirement that every active player has confirmed. I should be able to start the league regardless."* `tdc_generate_league_fixtures` now refuses only on fewer than two teams or a team with nobody on it. `private.tdc_league_ready` and `tdc_league_season_readiness` still answer the question and both pages still show who is outstanding — it is something a director is told, not something they are stopped for.
  - **The cutoff is lazy.** `tdc_apply_lineup_cutoff` locks any side that has not locked once `lineup_cutoff_hours` before the scheduled time has passed, and materialises. A separate call rather than something a read does quietly, because it writes — the page calls it on open. No scheduled job.
  - **After the lock a change of player is a reserve, not a new order**: `tdc_swap_frame_player` changes a frame's participant, keeps the frame and its slot, refuses a settled frame, and will not let a captain reach into the opposition's seat.
  - Also `tdc_set_match_squad`, `tdc_set_lineup_slot` (which refuses anybody not in the squad, and moves rather than duplicates a player put in a second slot), `tdc_lock_lineup` (which tells the other captain — the design's manually triggered notification), `tdc_unlock_lineup` (director only, and only before the frames exist), and the reads `tdc_match_squad`, `tdc_lineup_state` and `tdc_match_card`.
- **How a player gets in (migration `20261023100000`).** A director adding twenty people by hand is backwards; UV, 23 Oct. An entrant now has somewhere to stand before they are in.
  - **Five statuses.** `interested` (name down, not in: no team, no fixtures, not waited for by readiness), `active` (in), `inactive`, `withdrawn` (their own choice), `suspended` (the director took their place). Nothing that decides who plays had to change — squads, captaincy, fixtures and readiness all ask for `active`, so interested and suspended are already excluded everywhere.
  - **`entry_mode` on the season**, the same three choices a tournament has: `ld_only`, `interested`, `self_confirm`. Plus `paid_confirms` (ticking paid puts them in) and `is_listed` (whether `tdc_search_leagues` shows it).
  - **Two different confirmations, and they meet in one place.** `confirmed_at` has meant "the player themselves said they are playing" since 22 Oct. The director confirming somebody off the interested list is the league accepting them — a different act. From `20261023100000` showing interest set `confirmed_at` too; **UV reversed that on 23 Oct (migration `20261023201147`)**, because moving the Name Down list into a league showed everyone as confirmed. Putting your name down no longer confirms you. What does depends on the season's two entry settings:

    | Entry mode | Paid confirms | Confirmed when |
    |---|---|---|
    | interested | on | the director ticks paid |
    | interested | off | the player presses "I'm playing" on the season page, once let in |
    | self_confirm | on | the director ticks paid |
    | self_confirm | off | at once: joining is their confirmation |

    A player the director adds by hand still confirms for themselves, exactly as before. Anybody putting their name down again after withdrawing starts unconfirmed.
  - **Being let in off the Name Down list notifies the player** (`tdc_set_league_entrants_status`, kind `league_entry_added`, "You are in …, confirm that you are playing"), **only when paid confirms is off** and only those still unconfirmed. With paid confirms on, the paid tick is the confirmation, so the player is not asked. Before `20261023201147` a let-in player was already confirmed, so nobody needed telling.
  - **The season page has a Name Down state** ("Your name is down … nothing else for you to do yet"). Before, a Name Down player was always confirmed and the page never needed one; without it they would have been shown an "I'm playing" button the database refuses.
  - Tests: `supabase/tests/021_league_entry_confirmation.test.sql`.
  - **`paid_confirms` confirms, all the way.** It was promoting an interested player to active and leaving `confirmed_at` empty — and doing nothing at all for somebody the director had added by hand, because they were active already. Both fixed 23 Oct: ticking paid on a season that confirms on payment sets `confirmed_at` too, for anybody not withdrawn or suspended.
  - `tdc_set_league_entrants_status(ids[], status)` moves a whole selection at once — confirming fifteen people one request at a time is fifteen chances for half of it to land. It refuses a selection spanning two seasons, and moving somebody to interested or suspended takes their team and captaincy with it.

  - **What a stranger can read (10 Nov, UV; migration `20261110094557`; DECISIONS 10 Nov).** Only the Season and Standings tabs open to somebody not signed in.

About

A few lines for the venue's page. 500 characters at most.

Where

The street address without the area or city, e.g. "C5, The Gantry".
The part of town, e.g. "Lonehill, Fourways".
From Google Maps: Share, then Copy link. The pin in the banner opens it.

Tables

What they are, in words, e.g. "6 x 9ft, 1 snooker".

Contact

Digits and spaces, with a leading + if you like. The handset in the banner dials it.
With the country code, e.g. +27 82 123 4567, or the banner cannot open a chat.

Links

Hours

A day with no times is closed. A closing time earlier than the opening time is the next morning.

Anything the times cannot say, e.g. "Closed on public holidays".

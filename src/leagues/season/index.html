--- layout: layout.html title: League Season description: "Confirm your place, see your team and the league." extraStyles: - /stylesheets/league.css extraScripts: - /scripts/match-settings.js - /scripts/league-season.js --- {% comment %} The page for somebody who plays in a league, as opposed to running one. At the top, the league itself (UV, 24 Oct): a banner with its picture, name, status and everything about how it is played, then its numbers so far. Then the player's own place, the teams (each player with their picture and a link to their profile) and who runs it. A league player has to confirm their own place before the league can begin; that button lives here, and the notification they get when they are added links straight to it. A captain controls their own team from here too: claim a confirmed player who is on no team, release one, name another captain, rename the team. Everything is one call each, and after every write everything is read again. Reached as /leagues/season/?s=, with &team= to open one team and bring it into view (a team's name on the fixtures page). {% endcomment %}

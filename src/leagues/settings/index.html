The rules belong to the league, not the season, so a change here applies to every season of it. The controls are generated from TDCMatchDef, exactly as on the new-league page, including the Team play group: team size and the rotation table (version 3 of the ruleset, 23 Oct). Saving calls tdc_update_league, which also re-freezes the new rules onto every match in the league that has not started — no frames, no locked lineup, no signature — and says how many matches kept their old rules because they had. Frames are never rescored. docs/DECISIONS.md, 23 Oct. {% endcomment %}

League Settings

Every season of the league shares this name.

Saving applies to every match in the league that has not started yet. A match with a locked lineup, a recorded frame or a signature keeps the rules it started with.

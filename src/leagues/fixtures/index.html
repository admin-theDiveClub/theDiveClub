Everyone signed in can read it; the league director gets the controls, in each fixture's menu. Generating is a round robin — every team plays every other, once or several times — and refuses until every active player has confirmed. That is what confirmation is for: a schedule built on people who have not said they are playing is a schedule that gets rebuilt. Everything is one call each: tdc_league_fixtures to read, and tdc_generate_league_fixtures / tdc_clear_league_fixtures / tdc_add_league_fixture / tdc_set_league_fixture / tdc_remove_league_fixture / tdc_create_league_match / tdc_detach_league_match to change it. After every write the list is read again rather than patched here. Reached as /leagues/fixtures/?s=. {% endcomment %}

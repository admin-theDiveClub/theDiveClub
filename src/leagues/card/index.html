--- layout: layout.html title: Team Card description: "The night's card: lineups, every frame, and the two signatures." extraScripts: - /scripts/match-settings.js - /scripts/league-card.js --- {% comment %} The captain's scorecard. A full recording surface in its own right, not management around the match page: a captain can run the whole night from this grid without /match/ existing at all. What keeps the one-page rule honest is that there is one frame set, not one screen — this page and the match page read and write the same frames. Three things on it, in the order the night happens: 1. The squad and the order, until the side locks. The order is one player per position (five in a five-man league); the night is built from the two orders and the rotation table at the second lock. It is a blind exchange — the other side's names are withheld by tdc_match_lineup until both have locked, so nothing here can leak them. 2. The grid, once the frames exist, grouped by session. Every frame with its two players, the result entered beside it, and a reserve picker on each unplayed frame. A seat left empty waits for a reserve or a latecomer until a captain signs, then is a walkover. 3. The two signatures, with what changed since you gave yours. Reached as /leagues/card/?m=. {% endcomment %}

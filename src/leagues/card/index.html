--- layout: layout.html title: Team Card description: "The night's card: lineups, every frame, and the two signatures." extraStyles: - /stylesheets/league.css - /stylesheets/league-join.css - /stylesheets/banner-table.css extraScripts: - /scripts/banner-table.js - /scripts/match-settings.js - /scripts/league-standings-def.js - /scripts/league-nav.js - /scripts/league-card.js --- {% comment %} The captain's scorecard. A full recording surface in its own right, not management around the match page: a captain can run the whole night from this grid without /match/ existing at all. What keeps the one-page rule honest is that there is one frame set, not one screen — this page and the match page read and write the same frames. In the order the night happens (UV's layout, 24 Oct): 1. A banner: the two teams with their shields, the card's state as a badge, and the fixture's details (league, date, time, tables, team size, reserves, sessions, frames). 2. The order, until the side locks (no "here tonight" since 18 Nov: everybody else on the team is a reserve). The order is one player per position (five in a five-man league); the night is built from the two orders and the rotation table at the second lock. It is a blind exchange — the other side's names are withheld by tdc_match_lineup until both have locked, so nothing here can leak them. 3. The sessions, once the frames exist. Each frame a row: the home player, an L (who won the lag) and a W (who won the frame) three-way switch, home · nobody · away, the away player; the session's totals under it. Tapping a player's name on an unplayed frame opens the reserve picker, and a reserve wears a badge. A seat left empty waits for a reserve or a latecomer until a captain signs, then is a walkover. 4. The result and the signatures, one section in the banner's style (UV, 29 Oct): the two teams and the score, every player's frames (head to head, a table per team, or session by session), then the signature lines, the changelog and the buttons. Reached as /leagues/card/?m=. {% endcomment %}

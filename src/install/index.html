Put The Dive Club on your home screen

It then opens like an app: full screen, one tap from your home screen.

Before you start Open thediveclub.org in Safari

    The app keeps its own sign-in on an iPhone: sign in once more the first time you open it.

    1. Tap ShareThe square with an arrow. In Chrome and Edge it is in the address bar at the top; in Firefox, tap ☰ first.
    2. Tap Add to Home ScreenScroll the list down if you don't see it.
    3. Leave Open as Web App on, and tap AddThe Dive Club is now on your home screen.

    The app keeps its own sign-in on an iPhone: sign in once more the first time you open it.

    Easier to follow? Open thediveclub.org/install in Safari for the steps drawn out.

    One tap, then Install.

    If there's no Install button above, use your browser's menu:

    1. Chrome: tap ⋮, then Add to home screenIt may say Install app instead. Then tap Install.
    2. Samsung Internet: tap ☰, then Add page to, then Home screenSome versions say Install app.
    3. Firefox: tap ⋮, then InstallOr Add to Home screen.

    You've opened this inside another app. Apps can't be installed from here, so open the page in your phone's browser first.

    Open in Chrome
    1. Tap ⋯ or ⋮ at the top or bottom of the screenIt's the app's own menu, usually in a corner.
    2. Tap Open in browserIt may say Open in Safari, Open in Chrome or Open in external browser.
    3. Then come back to this pageIt will show the steps for your browser.
    No menu? Copy the link and paste it into your browser.

Physics

Each slider starts at the site's value. Changes last until the page is reloaded and reach no other page. Under each: what it does, which way is which, and (for the physics) what the value means right now.

{% comment %} What each slider does (UV, 18 Nov: "so I can better understand what each is doing"): under each, a line on what it does, its two ends, and for the physics a live line (data-banner-now, filled by sandbox-banner-controls.js) saying what the value means now. Four labels renamed to say what they do; the keys in banner-table.js keep their names (cg grip, ks the cushion's loss, kg the spin it strips, wyKeep side's decay). {% endcomment %}
← slowerfaster →

How fast time runs on the table. 1 is real time.

← rolls farstops soon →

How quickly a rolling ball slows down on the cloth.

← long skids, wide curvestakes hold at once →

The cloth's grip on a skidding ball: how soon draw, follow and swerve take hold and the ball starts rolling. Also how fast side wears off, with "Side wears off" below. Only after the ball's first contact.

← livelydead →

The share of the speed into a cushion that the cushion soaks up.

← keeps spinkills spin →

The share of a ball's spin (side, draw, follow) that each cushion strips off. What it leaves is all "Side off cushions" has to work with.

← nonea lot →

How far side spin bends the angle a ball comes off a cushion, after the cushion has stripped its share.

← lastsgone quickly →

How fast side spin wears off on the cloth once the ball has hit something, as a share of the cloth's grip. Higher is faster.

← livelysoft →

The share of the push lost when two balls meet.

← straightcurves →

How hard side spin curves a moving ball, from its first contact on, while it still has side. It fades out below 2 balls a second.

← neveralways →

The share of pots aimed a little off on purpose, so not every shot goes in.

← thinthick →

How thick the table's cushion lines are drawn.

← nonefull →

How dark the balls' and cushions' shadows are.

← sharpsoft →

How soft the shadows' edges are.

← nonestrong →

The glow round the balls and the table's lines.

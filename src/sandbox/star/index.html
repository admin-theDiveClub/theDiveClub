Player star

Each spoke is a player's rate against your standard for it. The grey ring is the standard: outside it is better, inside it is worse. Nothing here is saved. Not linked from the nav.

Your standards

Standard: what you expect of a decent player. Starting frames: how many frames at the standard every player starts with, so a few real frames move the spoke only a little.

Drawing

How many frames before a star is drawn fully solid. Fewer frames, fainter star.

How many points above the standard reach the outer edge, and below it reach the centre.

A dashed outline: the same player with no starting frames.
For league players: a frame won or lost by walkover counts as played.

Compare

Two players on one star: the first green, the second blue. A league player appears here once added.

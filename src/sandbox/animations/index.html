Animation picker

Every move the dots play on the site, then a library of new ones to choose from. Mark the ones you want "In the loop": The loop plays them one after another. The marks are kept in this browser; Copy the list to hand it over. Not linked from the nav.

The loop

Everything marked "In the loop", one after another: about six seconds each.

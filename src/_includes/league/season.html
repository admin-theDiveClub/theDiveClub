{% comment %} The page for somebody who plays in a league, as opposed to running one. At the top, the league itself (UV, 24 Oct): a banner with its picture, name, status and everything about how it is played. Then two tabs (UV, 5 Nov): Teams (each player with their picture and a link to their profile) and My Season (the player's own place, and for a captain who is waiting for a team). Below both the nights, then the numbers so far (UV, 10 Nov). The rules are their own tab. Who runs it is in the banner. A league player has to confirm their own place before the league can begin; that button lives here, and the notification they get when they are added links straight to it. A captain controls their own team from here too: claim a confirmed player who is on no team, release one, name another captain, rename the team. Everything is one call each, and after every write everything is read again. Reached as /leagues/season/?s=, with &team= to open one team and bring it into view (a team's name on the fixtures page). {% endcomment %}
